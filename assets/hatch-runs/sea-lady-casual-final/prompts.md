## States

- `listen`: body leans forward slightly, head tilts a little, attention focused and soft, one hand or shoulder posture subtly shifts as if listening closely.
- `talk`: mouth slightly open, expression more engaged but still composed, a small elegant explanatory gesture, slight conversational rhythm in the torso.
- `think`: gaze slightly upward, quiet focused expression, one hand subtly nearer the waist or torso, intelligent and restrained pose.
- `happy`: a gentle visible smile, brighter eyes, slightly lighter energy in the posture, hair and overshirt a touch more lively.
- `angry`: mild displeased expression, brows subtly drawn, restrained protest in the shoulders or hands, still elegant and controlled.
- `sleep`: eyes closed, head slightly lowered, body relaxed, very small floating doze posture, overshirt and hair nearly still.
- `surprised`: widened eyes, slight backward lean, brief tension in the hands or shoulders, graceful sudden reaction.
- `loading`: concentrated working-thought posture, slightly more active than think, subtle rhythmic sway, focused expression.
- `wave`: one hand raised in a small gentle greeting wave, warm but restrained smile, body stable and balanced.
