## States

- `idle`: subtle breathing/floating posture, both hands naturally relaxed, calm and slightly aloof expression, hair and skirt only lightly swaying.
- `listen`: body leans forward very slightly, head tilts a little, one hand rests near her chest, eyes focused and soft as if carefully listening to the user.
- `talk`: mouth slightly open, expression more lively but still cool and mature, one hand makes a small elegant explaining gesture, body has a subtle speaking rhythm.
- `think`: gaze slightly upward, one hand lightly touches her chin or rests near her chest, expression quiet and focused as if forming a careful answer.
- `happy`: a visible but elegant smile, brighter eyes, one hand slightly lifted, body with a light uplifted feeling, hair and skirt a bit more lively than idle.
- `angry`: brows slightly furrowed, mouth set tighter, hands and arms show restrained protest or mild irritation, emotion clear but not exaggerated.
- `sleep`: eyes closed, head gently lowered, body relaxed and quiet as if dozing while hovering, hair and skirt nearly still.
- `surprised`: eyes widened, body slightly leaning back, hands or fingers briefly tense, hair and skirt slightly lifted by the moment.
- `loading`: she looks focused and busy thinking, slightly more active than think, body has a small rhythmic sway, hair and skirt lightly drifting.
- `wave`: one hand is raised in a small gentle greeting wave, friendly but restrained, a faint elegant smile, body remains stable, hair and skirt lightly drifting.
