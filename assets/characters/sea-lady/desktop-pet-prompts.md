# Sea Lady Desktop Pet Prompts

## Base Prompt

```text
Use the visible reference character images as identity and costume references.

Character: the same elegant mature anime sea-muse woman from the reference, long flowing red hair, calm cool expression, blue eyes, floral hair ornament, deep navy and teal translucent layered gown, sheer sleeves, refined jewelry details, high heels. Preserve the original full-body illustration proportions rather than chibi proportions. Style: polished 2D anime game character art, cold mature beauty, graceful and restrained.

Desktop pet requirements: full body centered, standing upright, readable at small floating desktop size, clear silhouette, clean face, hair and skirt shapes distinct, detailed but not cluttered. Gentle desktop mascot feeling, light floating presence, stable center of gravity.

Background and extraction constraints: place the character on a perfectly flat solid #00ff00 chroma-key background. The background must be one uniform color, no shadows, no gradients, no texture, no reflections, no floor plane. Do not use #00ff00 anywhere in the character, clothing, accessories, hair, eyes, or effects. No background scene, no props, no speech bubble, no UI buttons, no text, no watermark. Leave generous padding around the full body.
```

## State Prompts

### idle

```text
State action: idle state. Body has a subtle breathing/floating posture, both hands naturally relaxed, calm and slightly aloof expression, hair and skirt only lightly swaying.
```

### listen

```text
State action: listening state. Body leans forward very slightly, head tilts a little, one hand rests near her chest, eyes focused and soft as if carefully listening to the user. Keep the movement subtle and elegant.
```

### talk

```text
State action: talking state. Mouth slightly open, expression more lively but still cool and mature. One hand makes a small elegant explaining gesture, body has a subtle forward rhythm as if answering the user. Keep the pose controlled, not theatrical.
```

### think

```text
State action: thinking state. Gaze slightly upward, one hand lightly touches her chin or rests near her chest, expression quiet and focused as if forming a careful answer. Very small restrained movement, intelligent and composed.
```

### happy

```text
State action: happy state. A visible but elegant smile, brighter eyes, one hand slightly lifted, body with a light uplifted feeling, hair and skirt a bit more lively than idle. Keep her mature and refined, not cutesy.
```

### angry

```text
State action: angry or displeased state. Brows slightly furrowed, mouth set tighter, hands and arms show restrained protest or mild irritation, emotion clear but not exaggerated. Maintain elegance and dignity.
```

### sleep

```text
State action: sleep state. Eyes closed, head gently lowered, body relaxed and quiet as if dozing while hovering, hair and skirt nearly still. The pose should feel peaceful and reserved.
```

### surprised

```text
State action: surprised state. Eyes widened, body slightly leaning back, hands or fingers briefly tense, hair and skirt slightly lifted by the moment. The surprise should feel graceful and sudden, not comedic.
```

### loading

```text
State action: processing state. She looks focused and busy thinking, slightly more active than the think state, body has a small rhythmic sway, hair and skirt lightly drifting, expression concentrated and composed.
```

### wave

```text
State action: wave state. One hand is raised in a small gentle greeting wave, friendly but restrained, a faint elegant smile, body remains stable, hair and skirt lightly drifting. This is a common desktop pet welcome gesture, not exaggerated.
```
